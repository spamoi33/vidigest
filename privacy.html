Vidigest privacy policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026 · Version française

In short

Vidigest has no server and collects no data for its developer. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Data processed

Video transcript. When you click “Download”, the transcript shown on the video page is saved to your computer and nothing is sent anywhere. When you click “Summarize”, that transcript is sent directly to the AI provider you selected in the settings (for example Google, Mistral AI, Infomaniak, or your own Ollama server). It is sent nowhere else, and only when you ask for it.

API key and settings. Your API key, the selected provider, and the summary length and language are stored locally in your browser (extension storage). They are not synced and are only sent to the relevant provider to authenticate your requests.

Read aloud. When you click “Listen”, the summary is read by the voices installed on your system (Web Speech API). Vidigest does not send it anywhere. Your voice, speed, pitch and volume settings are stored locally. If you choose an online voice provided by your operating system or another program, that voice's publisher may receive the text.

History. The last 50 summaries (title, video ID, date, summary) are kept locally in your browser. You can delete them at any time with the “Clear history” button.

Third-party providers

Data sent to an AI provider is subject to that provider's own privacy policy and terms of use, which you accept when you create your key. Vidigest has no agreement with these providers.

Deletion

Uninstalling the extension deletes its local data. You can also revoke your API key directly with your provider.
